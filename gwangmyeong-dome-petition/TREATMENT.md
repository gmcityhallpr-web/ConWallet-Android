# 광명 돔구장 유치 서명운동 — Broadcast News Package treatment

Style: Broadcast News Package · 20.0 s · 1080×1920 (9:16 short-form) · 30 fps · no narration (the online Korean voice is blocked here; the package graphics and the score carry it) · Korean labels, English second lines · subtitles as a separate `.srt`.

**Brand.** This is the city's campaign promo, not a newscast, so the brand plate is the campaign itself — **광명 돔구장 / GWANGMYEONG DOME** with a `CAMPAIGN` dateline — not a fictional broadcaster. No city logo (none supplied). Accent lemon / gold / paper / ink as in the style; the breaking colour is never used.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Sting → one lead package → call to action.** Sting becomes the bug; one story in three full-frame graphics (what: the dome; who wants it: the survey; what's needed: the signature goal and deadline); a slanted wipe into an end card with the QR code. One story, so no rundown.
2. **Rundown of three "reasons" ticked off.** Rundown (dome / citizens / deadline) → three items → rundown ticked → QR. The rundown costs 4 s of a 15-s film and its rows can't be read twice.
3. **Countdown.** 3 → 2 → 1 numbers (50,000 seats, 82 %, 10,000 signatures) → QR. Punchy, but a countdown reads as an event, and "11.08" is the date that matters.

**Chosen: 1.** In 15 s the viewer must leave with *what*, *why it's credible*, *what to do and by when*. The QR card needs the longest hold.

## Logline and arc

Gwangmyeong wants the 50,000-seat sports-and-concert dome; 82 % of surveyed citizens agree; sign by November 8 — scan here.

## Benchmark

Evening-news package graphics and financial-channel split-flap boards (grammar only: plate → bug, strap that never leaves, one fact per screen, stamps). No broadcaster's logo, fonts, colours, idents or music.

## Cue map

120 BPM minor news theme under shots 1–4 (spiccato eighths root–fifth–octave, pizzicato bass on the beat, horn pad, low-passed 16th saw pulse, kick 1 & 3, snare 2 & 4); glockenspiel on the bar values; timpani on the hard cuts (4.5, 9.0, 12.0); glockenspiel on the four lights; flap clicks 12.1–12.8; stamp 12.05; silence 14.5–15.0; major lift + tutti at 15.0; held chord to the end.
