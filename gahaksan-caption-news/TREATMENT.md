# 가학산 수목원 개장 — Caption News treatment

Style: Caption News · 15.0 s · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 30 fps · Korean captions with an English line · no narration (sound-off first) · **graphics only**: no footage or photos (none with usable rights were available), so every shot is a ledger card. Fictional channel bug: **GREEN BRIEF**.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Hook number → the turn on the same number.** ₩12 billion spent (hook) → site size → five spaces → "and admission?" → the counter rolls back down to ₩0. The turn re-reads the hook. One fact per shot.
2. **List of three that turns on the third.** Greenhouse, gardens, forest… then "all free". Works, but three items undersell five spaces and the turn has no number to land on.
3. **Countdown of numbers.** 1,844 plants → 32,820 ㎡ → 5 spaces → 0 won. Punchy, but four numbers in 15 s on a vertical feed is a lot to read, and the plant count needs its own document shot.

**Chosen: 1.** The style's strongest move is a number that changes meaning: 120억 → 0원 is the whole story in two numbers, and the ending is the hook re-read.

## Logline and arc

Gwangmyeong spent ₩12 billion on a new arboretum — and you can walk in for nothing.

- Setup (0–3.5): the hook number rolls to 120억 on frame 4.
- Body (3.5–10): how big (32,820 ㎡), what's inside (five spaces lighting one by one).
- Turn (10–15): hard stop into silence, "입장료는?", white flash, the counter rolls from 120억 down to 0원; verdict and opening hours.

## Benchmark

Captions-first social news explainers (mid-2010s feed news).
- Learn: the caption as the shot; one yellow keyword; numbers always carrying a source tag; hard cuts on a grid; silence before the turn.
- Don't take: any outlet's logo, idents, fonts, colour ratios or layouts; the news outlets' photos (rights); the city's logo.

## Beat sheet / cue map (120 BPM, beat 0.5 s)

- 0.13 hook hit (stab + counter rattle 0.13–0.58) · 0.4 caption chunk 1 · 0.6 keyword.
- 3.5 whoosh + card slide · 3.6–4.2 counter rattle · 4.5 plot fills.
- 6.5 panels rise (pops 0.1 s apart) · 7.0, 7.5, 8.0, 8.5, 9.0 panel lights (glock pings).
- 10.0 music cuts to silence; question slam (click + thump only).
- 11.0 white flash, turn hit (major brass-like stab + timpani-like drum), counter rattles down 11.0–11.6; music returns in full, major.
- 12.0, 12.5 verdict lines pop · 14.0 bells · ring-out to 15.0.

Music: synthesised 120 BPM pulse — kick on the beat, hats off-beat, eighth-note bass, sixteenth marimba ostinato, off-beat vibraphone chords, glockenspiel on lights, brass-like stabs on the big numbers. Two silences: a short drop at 9.75–10.0 (hats only) and the full stop 10.0–11.0.
