# 가학산 수목원 개장 — Treatment

Style: Pictogram Motion · 15.0 s · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 30 fps · Korean titles with an English second line · no narration (music and type carry it) · subtitles = the title cards (`.srt` from the edit list).

## Three candidate structures

1. **Catalogue run → icon wall.** A hook title, the five theme spaces as five beat-locked cards, then all five return as one wall with the opening date. Clear, reads as an opening announcement, fits the style's native "one card = one item" and "icon wall".
2. **A day in the arboretum.** One figure walks through the five spaces from morning to evening, the palette moving from dawn to dusk. Warm, but 15 s is too short for a time-of-day arc on top of five places.
3. **Countdown to opening.** 5 → 1 with one space per number, ending on "OPEN". Punchy, but a countdown says "event is coming", and the arboretum is already open (10.28).

**Chosen: 1.** In 15 s the viewer has to leave knowing *what* (가학산 수목원), *when* (10.28 open), *what's inside* (5 spaces) and *the hook* (free). A catalogue does that in the fewest words; the wall gives the time to read everything at once that the fast run doesn't.

## Logline and arc

A new arboretum in Gwangmyeong opens its gates: five gardens flash by one beat-bar at a time, then gather into one green wall — free, open now.

- Setup (0–3.2 s): an empty grid draws itself; one leaf cell grows into the whole pattern; the name slams in.
- Run (3.2–11.2 s): five spaces, five hues, one pictogram verb each.
- Turn / ending (11.2–15 s): half a beat of silence, then the pull-back: all five tiles on one wall, rows stop one by one, the wall turns one green. The grid that began empty ends full.

## Benchmark

Broadcast sports-identity packages and transit-wayfinding pictogram systems.
- Learn: one modular pattern used for everything; a strict beat grid; type as architecture; hue = section.
- Don't take: any real event's pictograms, emblem, pattern art, colour names or slogans; the city's official logo (we have no rights; the city name is set in type only).

## Beat sheet

- 0.0 grid hairlines start (rim tick) · 0.4 leaf cell appears · 0.8 pattern fills, **title slam** (drum + chord stab) · 1.2 chip · 3.2 first card.
- Each card: beat 0 cut + hue accent; beat 2 key action (arms open / reach / quarter turn / step / crouch).
- 10.8–11.2 drums and bass muted (half-beat silence) · 11.2 pull-back hit · 12.0–14.4 rows stop on beats · 14.4 wall turns one green (big hit, ring out to 15.0).

## Cue map

150 bpm, 4/4, 9.375 bars. Key D major pentatonic.
- Bars 1–2 (0–3.2): woodblock pulse, a rising shaker, frame-drum + marimba stab at 0.8.
- Bars 3–7 (3.2–11.2): full groove — frame drum on 1 & 3, rim click on 2 & 4, shaker 8ths, marimba riff, plucked synth bass; per-card accent on each cut.
- 10.8–11.2: silence but one tick.
- 11.2–15.0: groove returns half-time, marimba chord swells, final hit 14.4 with ring-out.

## Type

Black Han Sans (Korean title, heavy), Inter Tight 900 (English line), DM Mono (HUD/meta). Korean is the big title, English the second line. No narration → no caption band; the `.srt` lists the title cards.

Reading time: five cards at 1.6 s are a pace-ladder run (STYLE §4 minimum for a card that must be read is 1.6 s); the final wall repeats all five names and holds 3.4 s for the full read.
